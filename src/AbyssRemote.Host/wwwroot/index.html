First time on this phone or tablet?

An iPhone or iPad has to trust this PC's Abyss Remote certificate once. Until it does, Safari warns about this page and the picture does not connect.

  1. Download the certificate in Safari and allow the download.
  2. Open Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and install the Abyss Remote profile.
  3. Open Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings and turn on full trust for Abyss Remote.

Before you turn on trust, compare the certificate's fingerprint on this device with the one in the Abyss Remote window on the PC. They must be the same.

Only pair devices you control. Never share the code or port-forward this PC.
